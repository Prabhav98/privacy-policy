Privacy Policy for Electro Bill

Effective Date: September 26, 2026
Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Electro Bill - GST Invoice Generator ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("Electro Bill").

2. Information We Collect

To provide invoicing, point-of-sale (POS), GST reporting, and stock management services, we collect:

  • Personal Info: Name, email address, phone number, and user roles (Shop Admin, Billing Operator).
  • Business Info: Shop name, shop address, phone number, business email, and GSTIN.
  • Financial & Transactional Data: Invoices, line items, totals, GST tax breakdown (CGST, SGST, IGST), and customer billing info.
  • Inventory Data: Products, categories, HSN codes, unit prices, GST rates, stock levels, and movement logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for business and application functionality, including:

  • Generating GST-compliant retail and tax invoices.
  • Calculating tax summaries and downloadable GST sales reports.
  • Tracking live stock inventory and movement logs.
  • Authenticating users and enforcing shop role permissions.
  • Securing your session via Android Keystore hardware encryption.

4. Data Security & Storage

All network communication between Electro Bill and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+. Sensitive session tokens are encrypted locally using Android Keystore (AES-256 hardware encryption). We do not sell or trade your personal or business data to third parties.

5. Account & Data Deletion

Users have the right to request the deletion of their personal and business data. You may submit a data deletion request by emailing us at support@jrrstrading.com. We will process and delete your personal data within 30 business days, except where retention is required for legal or tax audit compliance.

If you have any questions or data deletion requests, contact us at:
